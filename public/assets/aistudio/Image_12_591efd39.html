Bacterial Leaf Blight

Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae

visibility Key Symptoms

Water-soaked to yellowish stripes along leaf margins, wilting, grayish-white blighted leaves under humid conditions.

medication
Recommended Solution

Streptocycline (1 g/10 L) + Copper Oxychloride 50% WP (25 g/10 L)

shield
Preventive Care

Avoid excess nitrogen top-dressing; maintain 5cm water level during tillering.

psychiatry Chilli (Field 2)
warning High Alert

Chilli Anthracnose / Die Back

Colletotrichum capsici

visibility Key Symptoms

Circular sunken dark spots on ripening chillies, twig drying from tip downwards, black acervuli rings.

medication
Recommended Solution

Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 ml/L or Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1 ml/L

shield
Preventive Care

Ensure proper field drainage after unseasonal rain; prune infected dry twigs.

psychiatry Maize (Field 3)
info Moderate

Fall Armyworm

Spodoptera frugiperda

visibility Key Symptoms

Pin-hole feeding damage in whorl leaves, extensive ragged leaf edges, abundant sawdust-like frass.

medication
Recommended Solution

Emamectin benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L or Bacillus thuringiensis spray

shield
Preventive Care

Install pheromone traps (5 traps/acre) for early adult moth surveillance.

psychiatry Groundnut (Field 4)
info Moderate

Tikka Leaf Spot

Cercospora arachidicola

visibility Key Symptoms

Dark necrotic circular spots with bright chlorotic yellow halos on older leaves leading to premature defoliation.

medication
Recommended Solution

Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1 g/L

shield
Preventive Care

Crop rotation with millets & deep summer ploughing to destroy resting fungal mycelium.

psychiatry Paddy (Field 1)
check_circle Monitored

Paddy Blast / Neck Blast

Magnaporthe oryzae

visibility Key Symptoms

Spindle-shaped lesions with grey/whitish centres and brown margins; darkening at neck node.

medication
Recommended Solution

Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L water or Isoprothiolane 40% EC

shield
Preventive Care

Balanced fertilizer application; avoid late evening overhead flood irrigation.

psychiatry Chilli (Field 2)
check_circle Monitored

Powdery Mildew in Chilli

Leveillula taurica

visibility Key Symptoms

White powdery growth on lower leaf surfaces, corresponding chlorotic patches, upward curling of foliage.

medication
Recommended Solution

Wettable Sulphur 80% WP @ 3 g/L or Dinocap @ 1 ml/L

shield
Preventive Care

Dusting with sulphur during early morning hours with low wind speeds.
