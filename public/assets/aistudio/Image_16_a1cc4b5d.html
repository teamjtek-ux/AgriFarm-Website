Bacterial Leaf Blight

warning
Key Symptoms

Water-soaked to yellowish stripes along leaf margins, wilting, grayish-white blighted leaves under humid conditions.

vaccines

High Alert
Flowering to Pod Setting

Anthracnose / Die Back

Colletotrichum capsici
warning
Key Symptoms

Dark sunken circular spots on ripening pods; tip die-back of twigs drying from top downwards with black dots.

vaccines
Recommended Solution

Spray Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1 ml/L or Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 0.5 ml/L at first appearance.

shield
Preventive Care

Collect and burn affected twigs; seed treatment with Trichoderma viride @ 10g/kg seed before sowing.

Fall Armyworm

Spodoptera frugiperda
warning
Key Symptoms

Pin-hole and ragged feeding perforations on central whorl leaves with coarse sawdust-like fecal matter.

vaccines
Recommended Solution

Whorl application of Emamectin benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L or Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 0.5 ml/L.

Tikka Leaf Spot

warning
Key Symptoms

Sub-circular reddish-brown to dark brown spots surrounded by a prominent yellow halo causing defoliation.

vaccines

Blast / Neck Blast

Magnaporthe oryzae
warning
Key Symptoms

Spindle-shaped elliptical lesions with gray center and brown margins on leaves; dark neck rot causing chaffy grains.

vaccines
Recommended Solution

Prophylactic spray of Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L or Isoprothiolane 40% EC @ 1.5 ml/L.

shield
Preventive Care

Monitored
Vegetative Growth Stage

Powdery Mildew

Leveillula taurica
warning
Key Symptoms

White powdery patches on lower leaf surface corresponding with yellow patches on upper surface followed by leaf drop.

vaccines
Recommended Solution

Foliar spray of Wettable Sulphur 80% WP @ 3 g/L or Penconazole 10% EC @ 0.5 ml/L.
